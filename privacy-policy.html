Akshar Keyboard Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Akshar Keyboard is an offline Android input method. The app does not collect, store, or transmit the text you type, and it does not request Internet access. Text is passed to the focused app through Android's input method framework.

Android and the app receiving your text handle that text under their own privacy policies. Enabling a third-party keyboard gives it the technical ability to provide text to the app you are using. Use a keyboard only if you trust its developer.

This policy may be updated if the app's behavior changes. The current policy will remain available at this URL.
